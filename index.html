02
Step 02 · The funnel ramp

Low friction first. Then we tighten the screws.

Funnels start wide and easy so we can validate creative fast. As soon as we have data, we raise the friction stage-by-stage until every lead is pre-qualified before our team even dials.

Phase 1

Low friction

Single-step opt-in (age range + zip). Maximizes volume so we can grade creative within 72 hours of launch.

Phase 2

Mid friction

3–4 step quiz: homeowner status, project type, timeline, budget band. Drops tire-kickers without crushing volume.

Phase 3

High friction

Full landing page with hero, social proof, scope qualifier, and address. Slots go straight to our setters.

Phase 4

Pre-qualified

By week 4–6, leads arrive pre-graded. Setters dial only A-tier appointments. Show rates climb to 70%+.

Why this works

Cheap data first, then quality.

Most agencies launch a landing page on day one and burn $2k before they know which hook is winning. We earn the data with low-friction forms, then earn quality by ramping the qualifier.

What you'll feel

Week 1–2: volume. Week 3+: quality.

Early appointments may include a few that don't perfectly qualify. By week 3, our setters are filtering hard and you're seeing almost only closes on the calendar.
